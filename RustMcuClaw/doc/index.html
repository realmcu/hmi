Why CLAW

It doesn't just chat — it takes action

An ordinary large language model only replies; CLAW, on a tiny MCU, closes the full loop of "understand — decide — execute."

Command it in one sentence

Through Feishu, the onboard touch screen, or a serial terminal, give instructions as if chatting with a person — no special syntax required.

Truly operates hardware

Lights up the light strip, reads the temperature/humidity sensor, reads and writes the SD card — it directly drives the peripherals on the board and turns answers into real actions.

Keeps watch and keeps time

Sets scheduled tasks and continuously monitors signals — for example, it lights up to warn automatically when humidity is too high, with no need for you to watch it all the time.

Long-term memory

Each conversation is summarized and retained, so it still remembers past agreements and preferences the next time you meet.

Knows its identity

Through the role file, it knows exactly who it is and what environment it runs in, and it also recognizes you, the one it is talking to.

Powered by a cloud brain

Seamlessly connects to a cloud large language model, giving even a single MCU chip powerful language understanding and reasoning abilities.

The Core

The agent loop: the secret to complex tasks

Faced with a complex request, CLAW does not answer in one shot; it repeatedly "thinks — calls tools — checks results" until the job is truly done — and that is exactly what makes it superior to a plain language model.

1
Understand your requestCombines this sentence with its knowledge of the environment
2
Decide which tool to useRead a sensor? Write the SD card? Or turn on a light?
3
Execute and get the resultOperates real hardware and gets real feedback
4
Decide whether to continueIf not done, go back to the previous step and keep looping
5
Give a natural-language answerSummarizes the whole thing into one fluent sentence
